About HoloStudy LMS

HoloStudy LMS combines two study tools into one learning space for NCERT Class 11 & 12 (NEET and CBSE boards):

  • 🔮 Hologram Room — 124 figures · 276 sub-views · 1,624 labels. Each NCERT diagram is a real 3-D object you can orbit, zoom, explode and quiz yourself on. Not a flat picture — a rebuilt model.
  • 📚 Study Console — the full NCERT text (~600k words) as Listen (≈13,000 voice notes with karaoke highlighting), Read (page-by-page with re-read), Summary (confidence gauge + recall quiz) and an AI agent that answers from the chapter.

USING IT

  • Open a subject → chapter, then pick Listen / Read / Summary / Ask AI, or tap a hologram to open it in the 3-D room.
  • In the Hologram Room: drag to orbit, wheel/pinch to zoom, V wireframe/x-ray, Q quiz mode, L labels.
  • Install it (button in the sidebar, or your browser's "Add to Home screen") to use it like a native Android app, fully offline.

Text is NCERT's, bundled for personal study. Runs entirely on your device — nothing is uploaded. Progress is saved in this browser only.
