About HoloStudy LMS

HoloStudy combines two study tools into one quiet learning space for NCERT Class 11 & 12 (NEET and CBSE boards):

  • Hologram Room: 124 figures · 276 sub-views · 1,624 labels. Each NCERT diagram is a 3-D model you can orbit, zoom, explode and quiz yourself on.
  • Study Console: the NCERT text as Listen (voice notes with word highlighting), Read (page by page), Summary (recall quiz) and an optional AI agent.

The study space

  • Ten black-and-white themes, each checked to keep text at WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1) or better in Soft, Standard and High contrast.
  • Fonts for each part: pick separate fonts for headings, body, lesson text and numbers from 15 bundled fonts, or tap a pairing (Clean, Claude-style, Textbook, Easy reading, Accessible, Dyslexia-friendly, Chalk notes). Includes text size and line-height sliders.
  • Wallpapers with an overlay slider. Any wallpaper that would make text hard to read is flagged and the overlay is raised automatically.
  • Focus Mode (lesson + notes only), a Pomodoro timer, ambient sound (rain, brown noise, silence) and a warm light filter for night study.
  • No autoplaying media, no pop-ups, no notification badges. Progress is shown as thin bars.

Using it: open a subject → chapter, then Listen / Read / Summary / Ask AI, or a hologram. In the Hologram Room: drag to orbit, pinch to zoom, V x-ray, Q quiz, L labels. Esc leaves Focus Mode.

Privacy

Everything runs on your device. Settings, notes and progress are stored locally per profile and never uploaded. The app has no ads, analytics or trackers. Only two optional features use the internet, and only with your own Claude API key: Ask AI sends your question and the chapter excerpt, and Claude holograms send the chapter or section text (plus up to 3 figure pages if you leave that option on). Both go straight from your device to Anthropic's API. Your key is stored only on your device, and each hologram is saved locally so it is paid for once.
